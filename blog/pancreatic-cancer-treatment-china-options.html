Pancreatic Cancer Treatment in China: What's Realistic for Advanced Disease (2026)

Pancreatic cancer has one of the worst prognoses in oncology — but China's top centres run among the highest-volume pancreatic oncology programmes in the world. This 2026 guide covers the real pathways, the drugs, and where the honest fit check matters.

Pancreatic cancer has one of the worst prognoses in oncology. For a patient with advanced or metastatic disease, the honest expectation is not cure — it is months of effective disease control at a cost the patient can afford. China's top centres run the highest-volume pancreatic oncology programmes in the world, and the cost is a fraction of Western care. That is a real outcome, not a marketing claim.

The Two Main Cancer Types

Pancreatic cancer refers to two different diseases with different treatments:

  • Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). About 90% of pancreatic cancers. The hardest to treat, the highest-volume, the most aggressive.
  • Pancreatic neuroendocrine tumour (PNET). About 10%. Different drug portfolio, different prognosis, different pathway. Often has a better prognosis than PDAC.

The Borderline Resectable Pathway

For a patient with borderline resectable pancreatic cancer — a stage between clearly resectable and clearly unresectable — the top Chinese centres offer a neoadjuvant chemotherapy followed by a combined hepatopancreatic resection (the 'unpicking' surgery). This pathway is one of the reasons Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital is the preferred destination for pancreatic PDAC in the region.

For a patient at home who has been told 'not a surgical candidate', the Chinese pathway is a real second opinion. It is not guaranteed to be successful, but it is a pathway that is not being offered at the local level.

The Drug Portfolio for PNET

  • Everolimus. An mTOR inhibitor, NMPA-approved for advanced PNET.
  • Sunitinib. A multi-kinase inhibitor, NMPA-approved for pancreatic NET.
  • Peptide receptor radionuclide therapy (PRRT). Lu-177 dotatate, NMPA-approved for somatostatin receptor-positive PNET.
  • SSRI (somatostatin receptor immunotherapy) and octreotide. For symptom management and tumour control.

The PNET pathway is a different disease with a different drug portfolio. The prognosis is generally better than PDAC, and the molecular profile (chromogranin A, somatostatin receptor status) drives the drug decision.

The Honest Fit Check

  • Right choice: Borderline resectable PDAC where the local pathway is limited to palliative chemotherapy.
  • Right choice: Advanced PDAC where the cost of FOLFIRINOX is prohibitive locally.
  • Right choice: PNET where the targeted portfolio is not available or too expensive locally.
  • Not the right choice: A patient in hospice stage where the trip cost exceeds the benefit.
  • Not the right choice: A patient whose drug path is the same at home and in China, and the local provider is competent.
The bottom line: for advanced pancreatic cancer, the honest expectation is not cure — it is months of effective disease control at a fraction of the Western cost. That is a real outcome. The honest fit check is the piece that decides whether the trip is worth it — and it should be done before the booking, not after.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is China a good destination for advanced pancreatic cancer treatment?

For advanced pancreatic cancer, yes — the top centres run the highest-volume pancreatic oncology programmes in the world, and the cost is 30-60% of Western treatment. The honest expectation for a patient with unresectable or metastatic disease is not cure, but months of effective disease control at a fraction of the cost. That is a real outcome, not a marketing claim.

What drugs are available in China for pancreatic cancer?

The full portfolio: FOLFIRINOX (the intensive chemotherapy regimen), gemcitabine + nab-paclitaxel (the standard doublet), gemcitabine + NALIRIFOX, and olaparib (a PARP inhibitor for BRCA-mutated pancreatic cancer, NMPA-approved). For pancreatic neuroendocrine tumours (PNET), the full portfolio of everolimus, sunitinib, and peptide receptor radionuclide therapy (PRRT) is available. The drug portfolio is not a discount version of the Western portfolio.

What is the survival difference between China and Western treatment for pancreatic cancer?

For a patient with unresectable or metastatic pancreatic cancer, the median survival with coordinated FOLFIRINOX-based therapy is 11-13 months in the Chinese data, comparable to the Western data. The difference is not the survival number — it is the cost. A coordinated year of FOLFIRINOX in China is USD $15,000-30,000; the US equivalent is USD $100,000-200,000.

Do Chinese centres offer pancreatic surgery for locally advanced disease?

Yes — for borderline resectable and locally advanced disease, the top centres offer neoadjuvant chemotherapy followed by a combined hepatopancreatic resection (the 'unpicking' surgery). For a patient with borderline resectable disease at home who is not offered this pathway, the Chinese pathway is a real option. This is one of the highest-volume specialties at Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital.

What should I bring to the first consultation?

The biopsy pathology (if done), the imaging (DICOM on USB), the prior treatment history (every regimen, dose, cycle, response), and the English translations of any prior specialist reports. If a biopsy has not been done, the specialist will usually do a fine needle aspiration (FNA) as part of the case review. See the biopsy guide for the process.
