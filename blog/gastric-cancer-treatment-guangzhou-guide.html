The Surgical Pathway

For a patient with resectable or potentially resectable gastric cancer, the top Chinese centres offer the D2 lymphadenectomy (the standard of care) with or without neoadjuvant chemotherapy. For a patient with hereditary diffuse gastric cancer (HDGC), germline testing and prophylactic gastrectomy are offered. This is a pathway that is well-established at Nanfang Hospital and Sun Yat-sen Memorial.

The Honest Fit Check

  • Right choice: HER2-positive gastric cancer where trastuzumab is not available or too expensive locally.
  • Right choice: Locally advanced gastric cancer where the D2 lymphadenectomy pathway is not available locally.
  • Right choice: HER2-negative disease where nivolumab + chemo is not available locally.
  • Not the right choice: A patient in hospice stage where the trip cost exceeds the benefit.
  • Not the right choice: A patient whose drug path is the same at home and in China.
The bottom line: for advanced gastric cancer, the Chinese pathway is not a discount — it is the same evidence-based protocol at a lower cost. The honest fit check is the piece that decides whether the trip is worth it — and it should be done before the booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Guangzhou hospitals are best for gastric cancer?

The three main destinations: Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital (the highest-volume gastric oncology programme in the region), Guangzhou Cancer Hospital (the dedicated cancer hospital with a strong gastric team), and Nanfang Hospital (preferred for gastric surgery). All three are within the Pearl River Delta and take international patients.

What drugs are available in China for gastric cancer?

The full portfolio: trastuzumab (for HER2-positive gastric cancer), ramucirumab (anti-VEGFR2, for second-line), trifluridine/tipiracil (TAS-102, for later-line), and the PD-1 inhibitor nivolumab (for HER2-negative advanced gastric cancer). For HER2-positive disease, trastuzumab combined with fluoropyrimidine + platinum is the standard first-line. The portfolio is not a discount version of the Western portfolio.

Does China offer gastric surgery for locally advanced disease?

Yes — for a patient with locally advanced but potentially resectable gastric cancer, the top centres offer a D2 lymphadenectomy (the standard of care) with or without neoadjuvant chemotherapy. For a patient with hereditary diffuse gastric cancer (HDGC), germline testing and prophylactic gastrectomy are offered. This is a pathway that is well-established at Nanfang Hospital.

What is the cost of gastric cancer treatment in China?

For a coordinated year of systemic therapy for advanced gastric cancer, the typical range is USD $15,000-40,000 depending on the regimen. For a patient needing a gastrectomy, the surgery itself is USD $8,000-20,000 plus the chemotherapy. The US equivalent is typically 3-4× these numbers. See the cost comparison guide for the full picture.

What should I bring to the first consultation?

The biopsy pathology (with HER2 and PD-L1 status), the imaging (DICOM on USB), the prior treatment history, and the English translations of any prior specialist reports. If a biopsy has not been done, the specialist will usually do an endoscopic biopsy as part of the case review. See the biopsy guide for the process.
